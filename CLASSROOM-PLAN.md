# Classroom upgrade

Approved direction: preserve Normal, Details and Video views; add Teacher and Student roles, a useful 2D fallback, date-based positions for 1800–2050, and true-scale distances with visibility markers.

## Design principles

- Use words a Class 6 student can understand. Explain a new term where it first appears.
- Give each screen one clear starting action. Reveal advanced controls when needed.
- Put reading comfort before decoration: generous text, visible focus, large touch targets, clear contrast.
- A teacher should be able to begin a lesson without instructions from the developer.
- Separate observations from explanations. Ask students to notice, predict, then check.
- Label enlarged sizes and approximate calculations honestly.
- Avoid promotional copy, poetic chapter headings, jargon, and ornamental dashboards.
- Keep learning available on slow connections, offline and without WebGL 2.
