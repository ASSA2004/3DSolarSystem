# Asset provenance

The PNG planet illustrations and 2K JPEG/PNG textures were inherited from the
original assa2004/3DSolarSystem repository. Their original creators and licenses
were not identified in the checkout. The WebP files are resized derivatives of
those same assets, not newly sourced images. Verify their original terms before
redistributing them under a new license. The project's MIT license covers code;
it does not relicense those images.

Install icons are original SVG geometry rasterized locally by `sharp`.
The Open Graph preview uses original typography and drawn orbital shapes.
The README screenshot depicts the running application and inherited imagery.

Three.js and other packages retain their license notices in their distributions.
JPL data is transcribed with its source, epoch, units and limitations in
`src/data/orbits.json`. Lesson text is original; NCERT pages are linked for
curriculum context and are not bundled or copied into the offline cache.
