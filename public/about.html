A place to see why.

Prayog Lab is a free, non-profit science and maths tool for school classrooms. Students predict, change a model, observe the result and explain what happened.

Five labs cover the Solar System, Optics, States of Matter, Mensuration 2D and Mensuration 3D. Teachers can lead short activities; students can explore independently.

Privacy and offline use

No accounts, cookies, analytics or tracking are included. Theme choices and notebook readings stay in this browser. After the first online visit, wait for “Ready to use offline” before disconnecting. External source links need internet access.

Models, not the real world

Planet positions use approximate NASA/JPL elements. Optics uses the paraxial thin-element model. The water simulation assumes normal atmospheric pressure; particle size and motion are illustrative. A sphere has no exact flat net: its displayed gores are explicitly approximate.

Grade bands are suggestions. The school must align lessons to its own state-board curriculum. New lab lessons are in English; Tamil scaffolding is available for future teacher-reviewed translations. Solar lessons retain their existing English, Tamil and Hindi options.
